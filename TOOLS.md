## Finance
- **Banking**
    - **[Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/)**: credit cards, checking and savings accounts, loans, and digital banking
        - **[Discover](https://www.discover.com/)**: credit cards, banking, and lending, now owned by Capital One
    - **[Chase](https://www.chase.com/)**: JPMorgan Chase's checking, savings, credit card, loan, and investing services
    - **[Citi](https://www.citi.com/)**: global banking, credit cards, investment services, and wealth management
    - **Digital Bank**
        - **[Ally Bank](https://www.ally.com/)**: online bank offering checking, savings, CDs, and other banking services
        - **[Chime](https://www.chime.com/)**: fintech that partners with banks to provide mobile banking services
        - **[Happen Bank (formerly LendingClub)](https://www.lendingclub.com/)**: digital bank offering personal loans, savings, and other banking products
        - **[SoFi](https://www.sofi.com/)**: digital banking, loans, investing, and other personal finance products
    - **[Wells Fargo](https://www.wellsfargo.com/)**: banking, credit cards, mortgages, loans, and investment services
- **Crowdfunding**
    - **[GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com/)**: donation-based crowdfunding for personal causes, emergencies, and charities
    - **[Indiegogo](https://www.indiegogo.com/)**: crowdfunding for new products and tech with fixed or flexible funding
    - **[Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/)**: all-or-nothing crowdfunding for creative projects and new products, with backer rewards
- **Cryptocurrency**
    - **Tokenization**
        - **[BlackRock BUIDL](https://securitize.io/blackrock/buidl)**: tokenized fund that gives on-chain access to U.S. Treasury securities
        - **[DTCC Tokenization](https://www.dtcc.com/digital-assets)**: DTCC's efforts to tokenize securities and modernize financial infrastructure
        - **[JPMorgan Kinexys](https://www.jpmorgan.com/kinexys)**: blockchain platform for institutional payments and settlement
        - **[Swift Tokenized Deposits](https://www.swift.com/our-solutions/interoperability-and-innovation/digital-assets)**: Swift's work to move tokenized deposits between banks
- **FinTech (Financial Technology)**
    - **Digital Wallet**
        - **[Apple Wallet](https://www.apple.com/wallet/)**: stores payment cards, tickets, passes, and digital IDs on iPhone
            - **[Apple Card](https://www.apple.com/apple-card/)**: Apple's credit card built into Apple Wallet for managing spending and rewards
            - **[Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/)**: Apple's contactless and online payment service
        - **[Google Wallet](https://wallet.google/)**: stores payment cards, tickets, passes, and digital IDs on Android
            - **[Google Pay](https://pay.google.com/)**: Google's online, in-app, and contactless payment service
    - **P2P Payments (Peer-to-Peer Payments)**
        - **[PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/)**: online platform for sending, receiving, and processing payments
            - **[Venmo](https://venmo.com/)**: PayPal's mobile app for sending, receiving, and requesting money
- **Investing**
    - **Brokerage**
        - **[Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/)**: brokerage accounts, investment funds, retirement planning, and wealth management
        - **[Vanguard](https://investor.vanguard.com/)**: low-cost index funds, ETFs, mutual funds, and retirement accounts
    - **Market Data**
        - **[MarketWatch](https://www.marketwatch.com/)**: financial news, stock prices, investment analysis, and economic news
    - **Robo-Advisor**
        - **[Betterment](https://www.betterment.com/)**: robo-advisor that manages portfolios and offers financial planning
        - **[Wealthfront](https://www.wealthfront.com/)**: robo-advisor for automated portfolio management and financial planning
- **Payment Network**
    - **[American Express](https://www.americanexpress.com/)**: operates a payment network and issues credit and charge cards
    - **[JCB](https://www.global.jcb/en/)**: Japanese payment network and card issuer for credit and debit transactions worldwide
    - **[Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/)**: global network that processes credit, debit, and prepaid card transactions
    - **[UnionPay](https://www.unionpayintl.com/en/)**: Chinese payment network for domestic and international card transactions
    - **[Visa](https://usa.visa.com/)**: global network that processes credit, debit, and prepaid card transactions
- **POS (Point of Sale)**
    - **[North](https://north.com/)**: merchant services, payment processing, and POS solutions
