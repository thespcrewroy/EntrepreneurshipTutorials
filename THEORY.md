## Accounting
- **Bookkeeping**: recording a business's financial transactions, invoices, expenses, and taxes

## Business Administration
- **Diagramming**: creating flowcharts, org charts, and process diagrams
- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**: software businesses use to connect and manage their core daily operations
- **IT Administration**: managing a business's servers, devices, user accounts, and remote access
- **Market Research**: gathering and analyzing information about markets, competitors, and customers
- **Presentation Software**: apps for creating and delivering slide-based visual presentations
- **Spreadsheet**: a grid-based app for organizing, calculating, analyzing, and charting data

## Entrepreneurship
- **AI Agent**: an AI system that plans and takes multi-step actions to complete knowledge tasks for you
    - **Agentic Browser**: a web browser with a built-in AI agent that can navigate and act on pages for you
- **AI Research Assistant**: an AI tool that searches, reads, and summarizes sources to answer questions with citations
- **Business Model Canvas**: a one-page template for mapping a business's value proposition, customers, channels, and costs
- **Prototyping**: quickly creating early versions of a product idea to test and refine it
- **Whiteboarding**: collaborating visually on an infinite canvas with sticky notes, diagrams, and sketches

## Finance
- **Banking**: institutions and services that manage money, accept deposits, provide loans, and process payments
    - **Core Banking System**: the back-end software that runs a bank's accounts, deposits, loans, and transactions
    - **Digital Bank**: a bank or fintech that operates entirely online through apps instead of branches
- **Crowdfunding**: raising money for a project, product, or cause through many small contributions from the public
- **Cryptocurrency**: decentralized digital technologies used to record transactions, tokenize assets, and transfer value
    - **Stablecoin**: a cryptocurrency designed to keep a stable value by pegging its price to an asset like the U.S. dollar
    - **Tokenization**: representing real-world assets such as funds, securities, or bank deposits as blockchain tokens
- **FinTech (Financial Technology)**: technology used to provide and manage financial services
    - **ACH (Automated Clearing House)**: a secure electronic network used to move money directly between bank accounts
    - **Digital Wallet**: an app that stores payment cards, tickets, passes, and IDs for contactless and online payments
    - **Instant Payments**: electronic transfers that clear and settle between bank accounts in seconds
    - **P2P Payments (Peer-to-Peer Payments)**: sending money directly between people through an app
- **Investing**: buying, selling, managing, and researching financial assets to build wealth or generate returns
    - **Brokerage**: a firm that lets investors buy and sell stocks, funds, and other securities
    - **Market Data**: real-time and historical prices, news, and analytics on financial markets
    - **Robo-Advisor**: an automated platform that builds and manages an investment portfolio using algorithms
- **Payment Network**: a system that connects financial institutions, merchants, and consumers to authorize card transactions
- **POS (Point of Sale)**: systems used to ring up sales, process payments, and track inventory

## Marketing
- **AI Marketing Tool**: AI that generates ad creatives, marketing copy, and branded campaigns
- **Audio Editing**: recording, editing, and processing sound files
    - **DAW (Digital Audio Workstation)**: software for recording, mixing, and producing music and audio
- **Email Marketing**: sending campaigns and automated messages to subscribers by email
- **Graphic Design**: creating visual content such as social posts, flyers, logos, and layouts
    - **Desktop Publishing**: designing page layouts for print and digital documents like magazines and brochures
    - **Photo Editing**: enhancing, retouching, and organizing digital photos
    - **Vector Graphics**: creating scalable artwork such as logos and icons from paths instead of pixels
- **Motion Graphics**: animated graphic design and visual effects for video
- **Screen Recording**: capturing a computer screen as video for tutorials, demos, or live streams
- **SMS Marketing**: sending promotions and notifications to customers by text message
- **Video Editing**: cutting, arranging, color grading, and exporting video footage

## Sales
- **Creator Monetization**: platforms that let creators earn money directly from fans and supporters
- **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: a system used to manage a company's customer interactions, relationships, and data
- **Online Marketplace**: a platform where businesses and freelancers list products or services for buyers to find
- **Payment Processing**: accepting card and online payments and depositing the funds to a business
- **Product Research**: finding products with strong demand and profit potential to sell

## Supply Chain
- **B2B Marketplace**: a platform that connects businesses with manufacturers, wholesalers, and suppliers
- **Dropshipping**: selling products that a supplier ships directly to customers, so the seller holds no inventory
- **Process Mining**: analyzing event logs from business systems to discover and optimize real processes
- **Shipping Carrier**: a company that transports packages and freight
